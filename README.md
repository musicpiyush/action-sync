## 📖 How to Use action-sync
Using action-sync is straightforward. Follow these steps:

1. **Import Meeting Transcripts:** Start by importing your meeting notes. You can either paste them directly into the app or upload a text file.

2. **Analyze the Content:** Click on the "Analyze" button. Claude will process the transcripts and identify key points.

3. **Generate Jira Tickets:** Once the analysis completes, click on "Create Tickets." action-sync will convert important points into Jira tickets automatically.

4. **Review the Tickets:** Check the generated tickets for any needed adjustments. You can edit the details before sending them to Jira.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use action-sync without a Jira account?
No, you need a Jira account to create tickets. Make sure you have your credentials handy.

### Is my meeting data safe?
Yes, action-sync keeps your data secure. It does not store your meeting transcripts after ticket generation.

### What types of meeting notes can I use?
You can use text files or any meeting note format. Just ensure they are legible and clear for better analysis.
